Privacy Policy

App: Gravity 8×8 ToF Viewer (package com.mammut.gravity_3d_tof)
Effective date: 11 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the "Gravity 8×8 ToF Viewer" Android app ("the app") handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. It works entirely offline on your device.

Data the app processes

The app reads distance measurements from a DFRobot 8×8 Matrix ToF sensor that you physically connect to your device over USB. This measurement data is used only to display the live visualization on screen and is held in memory while the app is running. It is not stored, logged, uploaded or shared with anyone.

Personal data

The app does not request, collect or process any personal data such as your name, email address, contacts, location or device identifiers.

Permissions

  • USB host / USB device access – required to communicate with the connected ToF sensor. It is used only for that purpose.

The app has no internet permission and therefore cannot send any data off your device.

Third parties and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third‑party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
